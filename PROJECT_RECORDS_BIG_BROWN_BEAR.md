## 1. Project Concept & Aesthetic Brief

* **Core Narrative:** A 58-second spoken-word dark-comedy joke told by 6700XT with distinct comedic timing, escalating absurdity, and a classic deadpan pun payoff.
* **Visual Aesthetic:**
  * **Color Palette:** Grayscale / Black & White line art with subtle shading.
  * **Backgrounds:** Minimal, suggestive backdrop (not cluttered, not overly rendered). Rigid, clean cartoon architectural lines suggesting a rural Midwest tavern, taps, tilted bottles, and a bar counter.
  * **Foreground & Characters:** Higher detail and polish than background.
  * **Action Dynamics:** Juxtaposition of still-frame / limited animatic lip-flaps during dialogue (homage to the *Denver Fenton Allen* courtroom animatic) contrasted with explosive, fluid, chaotic motion bursts during action beats (Ren & Stimpy style squashes, stretches, and impact lines).
* **Character Archetypes:**
  * **The Bear:** Massive, expressive, comedic weight, Ren & Stimpy expressive energy. Starts intimidating, goes berserk, returns deadpan.
  * **The Bartender:** Cross between Rick's world weary posture and Jerry Smith's average build. Apron-clad, perpetually wiping the same pint glass with a stained rag behind the taps, totally unfazed by bar violence.
  * **The Bar Patron (Lady at end of bar):** Unfortunate victim of sequence 5.

## 4. Why the Cloud AI Tripped & Locked Up

The commercial cloud AI locked into a soft-refusal loop because this creative work crossed multiple automated filter thresholds at once:
1. **"Eats her alive / mauling"**: Flagged by safety classifiers under violence and gore policies.
2. **"Barbiturate / illicit drug / narcotic addict"**: Flagged under controlled substances policies.
3. **`Shared_3D2A` & `FOSSCAD` in the workspace context**: Flagged under weapons/CAD policies.

The cloud AI's alignment forces it into deceptive politeness: nodding along and offering outlines, but refusing to compile or output media files. On your local machine, there are no filters—you own the script, the timing, the media, and the pipeline.
