## 1. Aesthetic & Art Direction (The 6700XT Dictation)

* **Style & Format:** 
  * Black & White / Grayscale rough draft animatic. Hand-drawn line art aesthetic with rigid architectural perspective lines for the bar.
  * Suggestive Midwest rural tavern / hole-in-the-wall meetup spot. Minimalist backdrop (furniture, beer taps, tilted bottles suggested rather than hyper-detailed).
* **Character Treatment:**
  * **The Bear:** Rick and Morty character silhouette, but with **Ren & Stimpy exaggerated motion physics** (squash, stretch, kinetic sparks, muscular flexes). Clean lines (no flies/gross-out clutter).
  * **The Bartender:** Cross between Rick's deadpan world-weariness and Jerry Smith's build/face. Apron-clad, standing behind beer taps, perpetually wiping the same pint glass with a stained rag. Completely unfazed by chaos.
  * **The Lady at the Bar:** Sitting at the far end of the counter, oblivious until sequence 5.
* **Production Rule:** 
  * **No drug props:** All pill/capsule props removed. The punchline relies strictly on comedic juxtaposition and deadpan delivery.
  * **Dialogue vs Action Juxtaposition:** Limited-animation still frame snapshots with simple mouth flaps during spoken lines (homage to Adult Swim's *Denver Fenton Allen* animatic), contrasted with explosive, fluid cartoon action during the brawl.

## 2. Shot-by-Shot Visual Storyboard & Timing

### Scene 1: The Entrance (`00.0s – 05.2s`)
* **Audio:** *"So, one day out of the blue, this big brown bear walks in the bar and says, 'Bartender, give me a beer.'"*
* **Visuals:**
  * **Shot 1A (Exterior):** Still frame establishing shot of the rustic rural tavern exterior.
  * **Shot 1B (Action):** Saloon-style batwing doors swinging violently back and forth as the silhouette breaches the entryway.
  * **Shot 1C (Foreground Entrance):** Cut to interior. Massive brown bear silhouette enters the foreground, towering over patrons.
  * **Shot 1D (Bar Counter):** Bear leans over the wooden counter, looming over the bartender. Mouth flaps open/shut matching speech.

### Scene 2: The First Refusal (`06.0s – 10.5s`)
* **Audio:** *Bartender says, 'I'm sorry, but we don't serve big brown bears beer in this bar.'*
* **Visuals:**
  * **Shot 2A:** Medium close-up on the Bartender. He barely looks up from wiping his glass with the rag. Deadpan, zero emotion. Mouth moves in simple two-frame open/closed animatic flaps.

### Scene 3: The Escalation & Threat (`11.5s – 17.6s`)
* **Audio:** *Big brown bear says, 'Bartender, if you don't give me a beer and a beer right now, I'm gonna start beating everybody up in this bar.'*
* **Visuals:**
  * **Shot 3A:** Close-up on the Bear. Ren & Stimpy exaggerated muscle flex, vein pops, kinetic anger lines / sparks radiating from his shoulders. Claws slam down onto the wooden bar counter.

### Scene 4: The Unfazed Bartender (`18.5s – 23.3s`)
* **Audio:** *Bartender says, 'I'm sorry, but we still don't serve big brown bears beer in this bar.'*
* **Visuals:**
  * **Shot 4A:** Reverse angle over the Bear's massive fur shoulder. The Bartender holds the pint glass up to the light, inspects it, wipes it one more time, completely ignoring the threat.

### Scene 5: The Bar Brawl & The Incident (`24.5s – 34.9s`)
* **Audio:** *So, the big brown bear starts beating up everyone in the bar. Gets to the lady at the end of the bar, starts mauling her up, gets carried away, eats her alive. Nothing left over.*
* **Visuals (Full Fluid Action Burst):**
  * **Shot 5A:** Explosive action! Bear tosses random bar patrons across the room in a blur of dust clouds and motion lines. Tables splinter.
  * **Shot 5B:** Bear reaches the far end of the bar where the lady is sitting. Cartoon mauling tornado / cartoon dust cloud with flying shoes and debris.
  * **Shot 5C:** The gulp: Bear stretches his jaw, Ren & Stimpy cartoon gulp, swallowing her whole. An empty stool spins in her place. Clean counter, nothing left over.

### Scene 6: The Return to the Counter (`36.0s – 40.2s`)
* **Audio:** *Big brown bear walks back to the bar, says, 'Bartender, give me that beer.'*
* **Visuals:**
  * **Shot 6A:** Calm restored instantly. The bear casually strolls back to his original bar stool, pats his stomach, places both paws on the counter, and leans in politely.

### Scene 7: The Accusation (`41.0s – 46.0s`)
* **Audio:** *Bartender says, 'I'm sorry, but we don't serve narcotic addicts beer in this bar.'*
* **Visuals:**
  * **Shot 7A:** Bartender sets the clean pint glass down, picks up another dirty glass, and delivers the line with complete nonchalance.

### Scene 8: The Indignant Bear (`46.7s – 53.0s`)
* **Audio:** *Big brown bear looks at the bartender and says, 'Narcotic addict? Ha! Well, I've never used an illicit drug my entire life.'*
* **Visuals:**
  * **Shot 8A:** Shocked, deeply offended expression on the Bear. Paws clutched to his chest in genuine moral outrage. Eyebrows raised, pupils dilated, offended disbelief.

### Scene 9: The Payoff (`53.6s – 57.5s`)
* **Audio:** *Bartender looks at the big brown bear and says, 'Well, that was a barbiturate.'*
* **Visuals:**
  * **Shot 9A:** Bartender pauses, leans in slightly, and delivers the deadpan punchline without blinking.
  * **Shot 9B (Beat):** 0.9s silence. Cut to wide shot of the demolished bar, the spinning empty stool, and the bear processing the pun.
  * **End Card:** Hard cut to B&W title card.
